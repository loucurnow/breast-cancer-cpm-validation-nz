\texttt{age.start.in} & Patient age at diagnosis (years) & Direct assignment from Age At Diagnosis \\
\texttt{screen.in} & Screening detection status & 1 if ReferralSource is 'BreastScreen Aotearoa' or 'Screen detected - non BSA'; 0 if A&E/Emergency/GP symptomatic; 9 otherwise. \\
\texttt{size.in} & Maximum invasive tumour size (mm) & Max InvasiveTumourSize per case \\
\texttt{grade.in} & Maximum histological tumour grade & Max HistologicalInvasiveCancerGrade per case \\
\texttt{nodes.in} & Number of positive lymph nodes & Direct mapping fromNumberOfNodesInvolvedByTumour. \\
\texttt{er.in} & Oestrogen Receptor status & Mapped from HP Oestrogen Result (Positive = 1, Negative = 0). \\
\texttt{her2.in} & HER2 receptor status & Combined IHC and FISH priority mapping (1 = positive, 0 = negative, 9 = unknown). \\
\texttt{ki67.in} & Ki-67 proliferation index status & Assigned 9 (unknown) due to low availability in baseline dataset. \\
\texttt{pr.in} & Progesterone Receptor status & Mapped from HP Progesterone Result (Positive = 1, Negative = 0). \\
\texttt{generation.in} & Chemotherapy generation level & Derived from chemo\_type string matching: 3 if anthracycline and taxane present; else 0. \\
\texttt{horm.in} & Adjuvant hormone therapy indicator & 1 if AdjuvantHormoneTherapy = 'Yes', 0 otherwise \\
\texttt{traz.in} & Adjuvant Trastuzumab therapy indicator & 1 if any HormoneTherapy = Trastuzumab, 0 otherwise \\
\texttt{bis.in} & Bisphosphonate therapy indicator & Hardcoded default assignment to 0. \\
\texttt{radio.in} & Adjuvant radiotherapy indicator & 1 if AdjuvantRadiationTherapy = 'Yes', 0 otherwise \\
\texttt{heart.gy.in} & Total radiation dose to the heart (Gray) & Sum of Total Dose records where Timing = Adjuvant. \\
\texttt{smoker.in} & Smoking status indicator & 1 if SmokingStatus = 'Current' or 'Non-smoker exposed to tobacco smoke', 0 otherwise. \\
\texttt{unique\_key} & Field description pending & Direct extraction from raw data. \\
\texttt{diag.year} & Year of tissue diagnosis & Extracted year component from DateOfTissueDiagnosis. \\
\texttt{ethnic} & Patient ethnic group & EthnicityLevel1 from demographic records. Maori, Pacific, Asian, European mapped directly. Remaining ethnicities grouped as 'Other'. \\
\texttt{menopause} & Menopausal status at diagnosis & Direct extraction from demographic records. \\
\texttt{Region} & Geographic treatment region & Direct extraction from source record sets. \\
\texttt{dod} & Date of death & Direct extraction from Demographics data. \\
\texttt{cod} & Cause of death & Direct extraction from Demographics data. \\
\texttt{final\_survival\_sweep} & Final follow-up or censoring date & DateOfDeath if exists, otherwise LastFollowupDate. \\
\texttt{surv.time.actual} & Actual follow-up time (days) & Minimum out of days.to.censor and days.to.death. \\
\texttt{days.to.censor} & Days from diagnosis to censoring sweep & Difference between final sweep and diagnosis date. \\
\texttt{days.to.death} & Days from diagnosis to death & Difference between death date and diagnosis date. \\
\texttt{surv.actual} & Survival status indicator & 0 if DateOfDeath is available, 1 otherwise. \\
\texttt{treatments.given} & Concatenated systemic and local treatment string & Letter codes appended based on binary indicators for adjuvant hormone, trastuzumab, bisphosphonate, radiotherapy, chemotherapy. \\
